## Sonic vs. Oscillating: Which Is Better?

This is the great toothbrush debate:

- **Sonic** (Philips Sonicare) — Side-to-side vibration at 31,000+ strokes/minute. Creates fluid dynamics that clean slightly beyond the bristle tips
- **Oscillating-rotating** (Oral-B) — Small round head rotates back and forth. More intuitive "polishing" feeling

**The evidence:** Clinical studies show both are equally effective at plaque removal and gingivitis reduction. The best type is whichever one you'll actually use consistently.

## Features That Actually Matter

After testing 12 models, here's what separates good from bad:

1. **Pressure sensor** — Essential. Prevents brushing too hard and damaging enamel/gums
2. **2-minute timer** — Ensures you brush long enough. Most people only brush 45 seconds without one
3. **Battery life** — 1+ week minimum. Nobody wants to charge a toothbrush every 2 days
4. **Brush head compatibility** — Cheaper replacement heads save money long-term

**Features that DON'T matter much:**
- Bluetooth/app connectivity (most people stop using it within a month)
- More than 3 cleaning modes (you'll use 1-2)
- UV sanitizer stands (clinical evidence is weak)
